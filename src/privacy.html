Browser-based enquiry preparation

The enquiry form prepares text in your browser. It does not submit your details to a website backend or claim that a message has been sent. Editing your details invalidates the previous draft until you prepare a new one.

Email and external communication

Opening an email draft passes your enquiry to your device’s email app. Review the text before sending. Telephone and WhatsApp links open external services, whose privacy practices apply. BPI handles the messages you send through those services.

Storage and analytics

The website does not add tracking cookies, analytics scripts or local storage for enquiry details. The hosting provider may keep normal technical access logs. The search feature works with a local page index and sends no search queries to a third-party search service.
